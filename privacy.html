Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

"n8n email tools" is a personal-use app. Its only user is the owner of the Gmail account it connects to. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API scopes gmail.readonly (read messages, threads and labels) and gmail.modify (change labels, archive, mark read or unread, and create drafts). It never sends, forwards or permanently deletes email, and never downloads attachments.

How data is used

Email data is processed by a self-hosted n8n instance on the owner's own computer, only when the owner asks for it through their own tools. Summaries are generated by a language model running on that same computer. Where the owner chooses to connect a chat application of their own, the results the owner requests are passed to that application.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the local n8n instance. The app does not keep copies of email content beyond what is needed to answer a request.

Sharing

Email data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.
